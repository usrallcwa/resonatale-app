Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2025

ResonaTale ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website and service at resonatale.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use our AI-powered film creation platform.

We are committed to complying with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK Data Protection Act 2018, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and all other applicable data protection laws.

1. Data Controller

ResonaTale is the data controller responsible for your personal data. For any privacy-related inquiries, contact us at:

Email: admin@resonatale.com

2. What Data We Collect

2.1 Data You Provide

  • Account information: email address and password (hashed, never stored in plain text)
  • Facial image: photo you upload to appear in your film
  • Voice recording: audio sample you record or upload for voice cloning
  • Story content: text prompts, mood selections, language preferences, and film duration choices
  • Payment information: processed securely by Stripe — we never store your card details

2.2 Data Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, session duration
  • Device data: browser type, operating system, screen resolution
  • IP address: used for security verification (Cloudflare Turnstile) and fraud prevention

2.3 Cookies

We use only essential cookies required for authentication and security. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Third-party services (Cloudflare, Stripe) may set their own essential cookies.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To create your personalized AI films (facial image + voice + story)
  • To process payments and manage your credit balance
  • To authenticate your account and secure your sessions
  • To prevent abuse and verify human users (Cloudflare Turnstile)
  • To send transactional emails (film ready notifications, password resets)
  • To improve our service based on aggregated, anonymized usage patterns

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)): processing necessary to provide the film creation service you requested
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): for processing your facial image and voice recording — you explicitly consent when uploading
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): for security, fraud prevention, and service improvement
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): for tax records and legal compliance

5. Biometric and Sensitive Data

Your facial image and voice recording are classified as biometric data under GDPR Article 9. We process this data solely based on your explicit consent, given when you upload your photo and voice sample. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

  • Facial images are used only to generate your personalized film
  • Voice recordings are used only to create AI voiceover in your voice
  • We do not use your biometric data for identification, surveillance, or any purpose other than film creation
  • Biometric data is deleted within 30 days of film completion, or immediately upon account deletion

6. Third-Party Services

We share data with the following processors, all of whom are contractually bound to protect your data:

  • OpenAI (United States) — generates screenplay and scene descriptions from your text prompts. Your facial image and voice are never sent to OpenAI.
  • Shotstack (Australia) — video rendering and composition. Receives scene images and audio to produce your final film.
  • ElevenLabs (United States) — voice synthesis. Receives your voice sample to generate narration.
  • Cloudflare (United States) — hosting, CDN, security, and bot verification (Turnstile).
  • Stripe (United States) — payment processing. We never see or store your card details.

International Transfers

Some processors are located outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Transfers to the United States are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and/or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, where applicable. We ensure all international transfers comply with GDPR Chapter V requirements.

7. Data Retention

  • Account data: retained while your account is active, deleted within 30 days of account deletion
  • Facial images and voice recordings: deleted within 30 days of film completion, or immediately upon account deletion
  • Generated films: stored for 90 days after creation, then automatically deleted unless you download them
  • Payment records: retained for 7 years as required by tax and financial regulations
  • Security logs: retained for 90 days, then automatically purged

8. Your Rights

Under GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA, you have the following rights:

  • Access: request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate personal data
  • Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Restriction: limit how we process your data
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw consent for biometric data processing at any time
  • Non-discrimination (CCPA): we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights

To exercise any of these rights, email admin@resonatale.com. We will respond within 30 days (GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA).

9. Data Security

  • All data transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.3)
  • Passwords hashed using bcrypt with salt
  • API keys and secrets stored as encrypted environment variables
  • Infrastructure hosted on Cloudflare with enterprise-grade DDoS protection
  • Access to production systems restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Regular security reviews and dependency audits

10. Children's Privacy

ResonaTale is not intended for users under 16 years of age (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us immediately at admin@resonatale.com and we will delete it promptly.

11. Automated Decision-Making

Our AI generates creative content (screenplays, images, voiceover, video) based on your inputs. This automated processing does not produce legal or similarly significant effects on you. The output is creative content that you review before any payment is made (free preview).

12. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach, we will:

  • Notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours where required by GDPR
  • Notify affected users without undue delay if the breach poses a high risk to their rights
  • Document all breaches and remedial actions taken

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email to registered users and by updating the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance.

14. Supervisory Authority

If you are in the EEA or UK and believe we have not adequately addressed your data protection concerns, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. A list of EU authorities is available at edpb.europa.eu.
