Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 14, 2026

🔒 Your Privacy Matters

ResonaTale is committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy rights under GDPR (EU) and applicable US privacy laws.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ResonaTale ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use our AI-powered film generation service.

Contact: admin@resonatale.com

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, name (optional), password (encrypted)
  • Photos: Images you upload for film generation
  • Voice Recordings: Audio recordings for voice cloning (minimum 30 seconds)
  • Story Details: Text descriptions, genre preferences, language selection
  • Payment Information: Processed securely by Stripe (we never store card details)

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent on Service
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, device type, operating system
  • Cookies: Session cookies for authentication and functionality
  • Security Data: Cloudflare Turnstile verification tokens

2.3 AI-Generated Data

  • Voice Models: AI-generated voice profiles created from your recordings
  • Generated Films: Videos created by processing your photos and voice

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Primary Purposes

  • Service Delivery: Generate personalized AI films using your photos and voice
  • Account Management: Create and maintain your account, process authentication
  • Payment Processing: Handle credit purchases and film generation payments
  • Communication: Send service updates, transaction confirmations, support responses

3.2 Legal Basis (GDPR - EU Users)

  • Contract Performance: Processing necessary to provide the Service you requested
  • Consent: Voice cloning and photo processing with your explicit consent
  • Legitimate Interest: Fraud prevention, security, service improvement
  • Legal Obligation: Tax compliance, response to legal requests

4. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

4.1 Service Providers

  • Cloudflare: Hosting, CDN, DDoS protection, Workers compute platform
  • Cloudflare R2: Secure file storage for photos, voice, and generated films
  • Cloudflare D1: Database for user accounts and transaction records
  • ElevenLabs: AI voice cloning and speech synthesis
  • OpenAI: AI script generation and creative content
  • Runway ML: AI video generation from images
  • Stripe: Payment processing (Stripe handles all payment data directly)

4.2 Data Transfers

EU Users: Your data is stored in EU data centers (Cloudflare's EU region). Third-party AI services (ElevenLabs, OpenAI, Runway) may process data in the US under Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission.

US Users: Data is stored in US data centers and processed domestically.

4.3 We Never Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Photos & Voice: Deleted 30 days after film generation (unless you save them)
  • Generated Films: Available for download for 90 days, then deleted
  • Payment Records: Retained for 7 years for tax compliance
  • Logs: Retained for 90 days for security and debugging

6. Your Privacy Rights

6.1 Rights for All Users

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails

6.2 Additional EU Rights (GDPR)

  • Data Portability: Receive your data in machine-readable format (JSON)
  • Restriction: Limit processing of your data
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Automated Decisions: Right not to be subject to purely automated decisions (not applicable - no automated decisions made)
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for voice cloning at any time
  • Lodge Complaint: File a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority

6.3 California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have additional rights:

  • Know what personal information is collected, used, shared
  • Delete personal information (with exceptions)
  • Opt-out of sale (we don't sell data)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising rights

6.4 How to Exercise Your Rights

Email us at admin@resonatale.com with your request. We will respond within:

  • EU Users: 30 days (GDPR requirement)
  • US Users: 45 days (extendable to 90 days)

7. Security Measures

  • Encryption: All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Authentication: Password hashing with bcrypt, JWT tokens for sessions
  • Access Control: Role-based access, principle of least privilege
  • Bot Protection: Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent automated abuse
  • Monitoring: 24/7 security monitoring and intrusion detection
  • Backups: Daily encrypted backups with 30-day retention

8. Cookies and Tracking

8.1 Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Session authentication, security (required for Service)
  • Performance Cookies: Error tracking, usage analytics (optional)

8.2 Cookie Management

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may limit Service functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

Age Requirement: Our Service is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us data, contact admin@resonatale.com immediately.

10. International Data Transfers

EU to US Transfers: When your data is processed by US-based AI providers (OpenAI, Runway, ElevenLabs), transfers are protected by:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
  • Additional safeguards (encryption, access controls)
  • EU-US Data Privacy Framework compliance (where applicable)

11. AI and Biometric Data

11.1 Voice Biometrics

Your voice recordings are considered biometric data under some regulations. We:

  • Obtain explicit consent before voice cloning
  • Use voice data solely for Service delivery
  • Do not share voice models with third parties
  • Delete voice recordings 30 days after use

11.2 Facial Recognition

We do not use facial recognition technology. Photos are processed only to generate stylized AI video content, not to identify individuals.

12. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information:

  • EU Users: We will notify you and relevant authorities within 72 hours (GDPR)
  • US Users: We will notify you in accordance with applicable state laws

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Material changes will be communicated via:

  • Email notification
  • Prominent notice on our website
  • In-app notification

Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
