Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Storybox is a storytelling service operated by ResonaTale. It is designed for adults who create calm, safe, personalized audio and video stories — including stories for children in their care.

1. Intended users

Storybox is intended for adults aged 18 and older. Children under 13 are not permitted to use the service directly. All stories for children must be created and managed by a parent or legal guardian.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the service:

  • Email address (used solely for account access and authentication via one-time login links)
  • Story prompts and generation inputs provided by adults
  • Generated audio or video files
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, and usage logs (for security and abuse prevention)
  • Payment-related metadata processed securely by Stripe (we do not store credit card details)

3. Voice, content & safety

Generated content is used solely to fulfill your requests. We do not sell, rent, or share your stories, audio, video, or voice data with other users. We do not use your content to train public or third-party models.

4. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process personal data under one or more of the following legal bases: your consent, performance of a contract, legitimate interests, or compliance with legal obligations, in accordance with the GDPR.

5. Children’s privacy (COPPA)

Storybox is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Adults are solely responsible for any content created for children using the service.

6. Data retention & deletion

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, and ensure security. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. Requests can be made by contacting us directly.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact: admin@resonatale.com
