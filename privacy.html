Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 7, 2026

ResonaTale ("we", "us", "our") operates the website resonatale.com and related services. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Data: Email address and encrypted password when you create an account.

Voice Recordings: Audio recordings you submit for voice cloning. These are processed by ElevenLabs to create a voice model unique to you.

Film Data: Story prompts, mood selections, language preferences, and generated films.

Payment Data: Processed securely by Stripe. We never store your credit card numbers.

Usage Data: Basic analytics including pages visited, features used, and device type.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and deliver AI-generated films
  • To clone your voice for film narration
  • To process payments and manage credits
  • To improve our service
  • To communicate important updates

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following services to deliver our product:

  • OpenAI — Script writing and image generation
  • Runway — AI video generation
  • ElevenLabs — Voice cloning and text-to-speech
  • Shotstack — Video assembly and rendering
  • Stripe — Payment processing
  • Cloudflare — Hosting, storage, and security

Each service processes data according to their own privacy policies. We only share the minimum data necessary for each service to function.

4. Voice Data

Your voice recording is used solely to create a cloned voice model for narrating your films. We do not sell, share, or use your voice for any other purpose. Voice models are tied to your account and are not accessible by other users.

You may re-record your voice once every 30 days. You may request deletion of your voice data at any time by contacting us.

5. AI-Generated Content

Films created through ResonaTale are generated by AI. You own the rights to films you create and pay for. We retain a license to store and deliver your content through our platform.

6. Data Storage

Your data is stored on Cloudflare's global network using encrypted databases (D1) and object storage (R2). Films are stored until you delete them or close your account.

7. Data Retention

  • Account data: retained until you delete your account
  • Voice models: retained until you delete or re-record
  • Films: retained for 1 year after creation
  • Payment records: retained as required by law

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Delete your voice recordings
  • Export your films
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

9. Children

ResonaTale is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

10. Security

We use industry-standard encryption, secure authentication (JWT), and Cloudflare's security infrastructure to protect your data. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at admin@resonatale.com
